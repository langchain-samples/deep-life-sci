# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue.** Report privately through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/langchain-samples/deep-life-sci/security/advisories/new),
or email michael.cunningham@langchain.dev.

Include what you did, what happened, and the commit you saw it on. We will acknowledge
receipt and tell you whether we consider it in scope.
